Support information

Use this mode when λA,B and ΔA,B come from numerical integration, a reported representativeness-error estimate, or another component-specific calculation. The example buttons use semivariogram mode whenever the corresponding parameter inputs are available.

Structural covariance model

Enter the structural partial sill that belongs to the structural range. It may be the partial sill of a response semivariogram or of a residual/component semivariogram; it is not inferred from the observed reference variance.

Equations

λA,B = Var{ZB(Bi)} / σ²obs
R²cor,B ≈ R²cor,obs / λA,B
MSEB ≈ MSEobs − ΔA,B − σ²ε
R²pred,B ≈ 1 − MSEB / Var{ZB(Bi)}
Δp,B ≈ σ²μ + 128/(45π) σ²S{1 − ρS(R)}
